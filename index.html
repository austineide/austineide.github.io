Austin Eide

Email: austin dot eide at pnnl.gov

I am a postdoc in the Computational Mathematics group at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory (PNNL). Before this I worked briefly in finance and was a postdoc with Paweł Prałat at Toronto Metropolitan University. I received a PhD in Mathematics from the University of Nebraska-Lincoln in 2023 under the supervision of Xavier Pérez Giménez.

Research

My research focuses on random graphs, randomized algorithms, stochastic processes, and network science.
